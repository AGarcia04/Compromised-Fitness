Compromised Fitness — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Compromised Fitness is a personal fitness-training project. It is currently used only by its developer. This page explains what data the app reads from your WHOOP account and what it does with it. If the app is opened to other people later, this policy will be updated first.

What data the app accesses

When you connect a WHOOP account, the app requests read-only access to: recovery (score, heart rate variability, resting heart rate), cycles (day strain, average heart rate), sleep, workouts, basic profile information, and body measurements (height, weight, max heart rate). The app cannot change anything in your WHOOP account.

The app also stores information you enter yourself, such as training sessions, meals and calories, body weight, and race or time-trial results.

How the data is used

Only to show you your own data and to build and adjust your own training plan. Your data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where the data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a database operated by the app's developer, with a cloud database provider hosting it. Access controls ensure each user can only see their own records. The developer can access the database for maintenance. Your data is not shared with other users or with third parties, except the hosting providers needed to run the app.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted or you stop using the app. To delete your data, email the address below and all of your records, including your stored WHOOP access tokens, will be removed.

Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time from your WHOOP account settings, which revokes its access. You can also ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion by email.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Not medical advice

The app provides general fitness information and training suggestions based on your data. It is not medical advice. Check with a qualified professional before changing your training, nutrition, or if you have a health concern.

Affiliation

WHOOP is a trademark of WHOOP, Inc. Compromised Fitness is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WHOOP.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
